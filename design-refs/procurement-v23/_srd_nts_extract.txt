1. Document Purpose 
This System Requirements Document defines what the development and implementation team 
must deliver for the Client’s Procurement Management System. 
This is an internal implementation and development document. The dates contained in this 
SRD are the technical team’s delivery milestones and should be used to organise development, 
configuration, deployment, testing, remediation and integration work. 
The system must provide a complete controlled procurement workflow from internal requisition 
through sourcing, evaluation, purchase order, goods/service receipt and invoice hand-off. 
The implementation will be delivered in two phases. 
Phase 1 – Standalone Procurement System 
The Procurement Management System must first operate as a complete standalone application 
on the Client’s infrastructure. 
The development team must have the application technically deployed onto the Client’s 
infrastructure by 22 September 2026. 


===== PAGE 2 =====
The standalone system must be capable of operating independently using its own: 
●​ users; 
●​ permissions; 
●​ vendor records; 
●​ approval workflows; 
●​ procurement records; 
●​ documents; 
●​ notifications; 
●​ reporting; 
●​ audit trail; and 
●​ administrative configuration. 
User onboarding will take place on 23 September 2026, followed by Phase 1 UAT on 24 
September 2026. 
Following successful remediation of any critical Phase 1 issues, the Phase 1 production/go-live 
deployment will take place on 25 September 2026. 
Phase 2 – Systems Integration 
Phase 2 begins on 25 September 2026, immediately following the Phase 1 production 
deployment. 
The first Phase 2 activity will be detailed SmartStream process and data mapping. 
Integrations may subsequently include: 
●​ SmartStream Accounting; 
●​ approved RPA processes; 
●​ authentication/identity systems where applicable; 
●​ email infrastructure; and 
●​ other systems agreed through the Integration Design Matrix. 
Internal integration UAT must be conducted on 27 September 2026. 
External UAT with the Client is scheduled for 29 September 2026. 
The core implementation principle is: 
The procurement platform must work correctly on its own before integration 
complexity is introduced.

3. System Objectives 
The system must: 
1.​ digitise the Client’s procurement workflow; 
2.​ reduce manual procurement administration; 
3.​ provide controlled approval routing; 
4.​ centralise vendor information; 
5.​ maintain procurement documentation; 
6.​ provide transparent quotation comparison; 
7.​ maintain complete audit histories; 
8.​ enforce user permissions and segregation of duties; 
9.​ provide management reporting; 
10.​support procurement policy controls; 
11.​operate independently during Phase 1; 
12.​integrate with SmartStream and other agreed systems during Phase 2; 
13.​support an optional AI-assisted procurement review layer; and 
14.​remain easy for Client users to operate without technical knowledge.

4.​ What approval is required? 
5.​ What happens after approval? 
6.​ What system receives the next transaction? 
7.​ What happens if that action fails?
