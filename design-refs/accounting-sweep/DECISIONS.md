# Accounting sweep: decisions

Where the SRDs disagree or say nothing (SRD_REQUIREMENTS.md §1 contradictions, §7 ambiguities), the reading built:

| # | Question | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 | Base currency (documents say ZWL, ZiG or USD) | **USD**, with ZiG as the second currency | USD is the system's default currency (`currencies.isDefault`); every ledger amount is already stored in it |
| D2 | Tenant time zone for scheduled work and the fiscal day | **Africa/Harare** when none is configured (settings → company profile → `FISCAL_TIMEZONE` first) | The organisation keeps its books in Zimbabwe; SRD UAT 8.4 requires tenant time, not server UTC |
| D3 | Who posts to the ledger | Preparers (accountant, finance officer, assistant) create; approvers (Finance Manager, CFO, administrators) post, void, reverse and run postings; nobody posts their own entry | SRD maker-checker and segregation-of-duties rules; the posting service already refuses the preparer |
| D4 | Short-term investment roles (SRD names a "Treasury Management" role that does not exist) | Treasury work = `accounting.treasury.manage` (Finance Manager, accountant, finance officer, CFO, admins); approving accruals, the posting mode and voids = `accounting.treasury.approve` (Finance Manager, CFO, admins) | SRD 3.1 / 3.3: the CFO decides draft vs approved posting; preparers set up instruments |
| D5 | When interest accrues | A scheduled run at 23:59 tenant time accrues every active investment through today, catching up missed days; it no longer runs when someone signs in (`STI_LOGIN_ACCRUAL=on` restores that) | SRD 3.2 / UAT 2.1: a daily batch at 23:59; a login-driven run booked interest only on days somebody signed in |
| D6 | Depreciation | Monthly, on the 1st at 02:15, for the month just ended; "Run now" also posts the last completed month; a month is never posted twice | SRD J6 (monthly, last-run date prevents double posting) |
| D7 | Official rate | Recorded daily at 09:00 from the RBZ/Zimrate source; if it cannot be reached, the last stored rate stays in use and finance is told | SRD J1 |
| D8 | Timesheet approval | Line management (`accounting.timesheets.manage`), not accounting preparation (`manage_accounting`) | Giving preparers `manage_accounting` must not make them organisation-wide timesheet approvers |
| D9 | Overdue receivables | Each morning the receivables team is told which invoices are past due; customers are not emailed automatically | The SRDs imply reminders but define no dunning letters or schedule; a message to the team is safe and reversible |
| D10 | Who may approve a day's interest journal | Anyone holding `accounting.treasury.approve`, including when the scheduled run recorded them as its actor | The journal is drafted by the system on a schedule, not prepared by the approver; maker-checker is about people preparing entries |
| D11 | A rate change dated before days already accrued | Days still in draft are recalculated at the new rate; if any of those days is already posted, the change is refused and names the first date it can take effect | SRD 2.5 (new rate from its effective date) without rewriting posted ledger history; a correction to posted interest is a journal, not a silent restatement |
| D12 | Currency of the investments dashboard | The reporting (base) currency, USD, at the as-of date's rate; per-investment figures stay in the investment's own currency | The CFO view is a portfolio total; adding ZiG to USD is meaningless |
| D13 | Approval steps fanned out to a role (every CFO) | The first approver on the step decides it for accounting executions (payments, investment bookings, chart-of-accounts changes), as procurement already does | SRD: CFO approval, not approval by every CFO account |
| D14 | May the CFO or an administrator post a journal they prepared? | No: nobody posts their own journal. Flows that raise and complete their own journal (sending an invoice, approving an investment's daily interest) keep an explicit exception in code | SRD maker-checker on every approval; D3. A CFO's journal is posted by the Finance Manager or another approver |
| D15 | Future-dated journals | Refused: a manual journal records what has happened, dated today or earlier (tenant time). Recurring schedules and accrual reversals create their journals on the day | The SRDs are silent; a future-dated posting would sit in an open future period and distort period-to-date reports |
| D16 | Voiding a posted journal | Only while its period is open; reports then leave out the journal and its reversal together. In a locked period the correction is a new journal in the open period | Locked periods stay as reported (SRD period lock); the existing reporting rule drops void pairs |
| D17 | How foreign-currency amounts reach USD reports | Base (USD) amount and rate stored on every journal line (from the journal's own rate snapshot, which every posting already records); reports read base amounts; revaluation posts only the movement since the last revaluation. Built in phase 5; until then foreign-currency activity is reported per currency and no revaluation is offered | SRD ACC-GL-04 / ACC-FX-05 require base amounts per line with the rate locked at posting; the per-currency books in place today cannot produce a correct USD report or revaluation | As built: the base amount is derived from the rate locked on the journal (every journal already stores its USD/ZiG snapshot at posting) rather than copied onto each line: the database account cannot create triggers, and a copy could drift from its journal; the result is the same fixed amount per posted line. |
| D18 | Unbalanced posted journals found by the integrity check | Dev/test data: voided with the reason recorded. Any other environment: never voided automatically; the check alerts, finance reviews with the owning module (payroll here) and posts a correcting journal | A void rewrites a period's figures; real books are corrected by a visible entry, test debris is removed |
| D19 | The tenant's clock when none is configured | Africa/Harare for everything (fiscal periods, "today", future-date checks), the same fallback the investment and scheduled-job clocks already used; a company profile time zone still overrides | The fiscal clock fell back to UTC while the others used Harare: between midnight in Harare and midnight UTC a journal dated today was refused as future-dated |
| D20 | Removing a journal | Never: a draft is withdrawn (VOID, kept with its number), a posted journal is voided with a reversing entry. Payroll's accounting rebuild and an investment rate restatement now withdraw or void instead of deleting | SRD ACC-AUD-01/02 and the sequential audit number: a deleted journal leaves a hole no one can explain |
| D21 | Inventory costing (conflict C24: FIFO in CORE tests vs weighted average in UIR 08) | Moving weighted average per item: a receipt moves the average, issues and count differences leave at it | One cost per item that the ledger, the valuation and the roll-forward all use; FIFO layers are not kept and the old FIFO/LIFO parameters return the average |
| D22 | When a stock movement reaches the ledger | Posted with the movement, in the same transaction (reference STK-…); corrected by another movement, never voided on its own. Count differences are booked by approvers | SRD ACC-INV-02 (perpetual: the entry in the same operation) and ACC-INV-06 (count variance approval); a draft journal whose stock has already moved would let the two drift apart |
| D24 | Who approves an employee claim, and when it reaches the ledger | Line manager (same department, or organisation-wide scope) → finance reviewer → finance manager, three different people none of whom is the claimant; the ledger entry is posted when the finance manager approves (owed to the employee on 2260 Staff Claims Payable) and cleared when it is paid from a bank. A missing receipt above the threshold blocks submission; possible duplicates and daily limits are exceptions approved only with a note | SRD ACC-EXP-03…07 and UIR 13; the liability exists once the company accepts the claim, not when the money leaves; a hard block on policy breaches would stop legitimate exceptions the SRD allows with justification |
| D23 | Which accounts hold VAT (SRD ACC-TAX-02 names 2100 / 2110; the chart has 1101 VAT Input Recoverable, 2100 VAT Output Payable and 2110 VAT Input (clearing) / VAT Payable) | Input VAT on 1101 (recoverable: an asset), output VAT on 2100; resolved in one place for every posting and report | The postings already used 1101 (46 lines, none on 2110); lookups by the name "VAT Input" matched both, so the report and the postings could disagree |
