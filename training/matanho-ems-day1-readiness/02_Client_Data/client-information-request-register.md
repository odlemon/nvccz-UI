# Client information request register

Return through the client-approved secure channel, not this repository. Do not send passwords or identity documents by ordinary email.

| ID | Request | Suggested source owner | Format / guidance | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | User names, work email, department, responsibilities, requested roles, approver, effective/end date | HR / sponsor | One row per person; no passwords; verify spelling and employment status. | Awaiting client |
| C-02 | Fund structures and legal entities | Finance / Legal | Include legal name, jurisdiction, registration reference (secure channel), base currency, relationship, effective dates. Import requirements not yet confirmed. | Awaiting client |
| C-03 | Investment mandates and approval limits | Investment lead / sponsor | Strategy, geography, sectors, stage, ticket range, decision rights, restrictions, review date. Confirm actual UI/API mapping before import. | Awaiting client |
| C-04 | Investor register | IR / Compliance | Legal entity/person, classification, country, contact owner, KYC status, data consent; transmit via approved secure channel. | Awaiting client |
| C-05 | Commitments and schedules | Fund admin / Finance | Fund, investor, amount, currency, commitment date, drawdown/distribution schedule and status; reconcile totals to an approved source. | Awaiting client |
| C-06 | Reporting obligations and calendar | Finance / Portfolio | Report, recipient, frequency, due date, owner, approval. | Awaiting client |
| C-07 | Current process and approval map | Process owners | Swimlane or interview; note variants and exception paths. | Discovery |
| C-08 | Day 2 investor-management participants and training goals | Sponsor / IR lead | Names/roles and preferred session outcomes. | Awaiting client |

## Submission control

For every file: record source owner, as-of date, version, approval, confidentiality classification, validation status, and secure-storage location. Keep the source file unchanged; validate a working copy. Never use real data in development until authorized and masked.
