- **Staff sign-in:** [https://dev.matanho.com/login](https://dev.matanho.com/login)
- **Environment:** Singapore development, not production.
- **API health:** [https://dev-api.matanho.com/health](https://dev-api.matanho.com/health) — diagnostic only; do not share API credentials or use this as a training screen.
- Ask each attendee to use an individually provisioned, approved account. Passwords and reset links must be delivered separately through the organization’s approved secure method; they do not belong in this folder.
- Before the session, confirm the attendee network can reach the URL, account works, role assignment is approved, and the displayed environment is dev.
- Do not enter real client/investor/financial data until authorization, masking, backup and restore controls are approved.
- Avoid the pending synthetic fund form until its POST state is reconciled. Current account shown in browser: Admin NTS (test account); do not assume this is a client administrator.
- Report a failure with timestamp, route, role, exact sanitized toast text, and a redacted screenshot; never attach cookies, tokens, passwords, or client records.
